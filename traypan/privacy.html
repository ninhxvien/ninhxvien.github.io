Privacy Policy — Marble Tray Pan

Last updated: 1 September 2026 · App ID com.nxvteam.marble.traypan · Published by NXV Team

Short version: Marble Tray Pan has no accounts, no analytics and no trackers of our own, and your game progress never leaves your device. The one exception is advertising: the app shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob uses your device's advertising ID to choose them.

What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. The app contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service, and none of our own code sends information anywhere. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files, and we have no server to send them to.

What the app does contain is Google's advertising SDK — see the Advertising section below for exactly what that means.

What the game stores on your device

So that you do not lose your progress, the game saves the following in your device's local app storage. It never leaves your device, and it is not readable by us or by anyone else:

  • which level you have reached, and your star rating per level
  • your in-game gold, win streak, piggy bank, daily-gift and event progress
  • your booster inventory
  • your sound and music settings, and whether you have seen the tutorial
  • which one-time in-app purchases you already own, so they are not offered twice

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android Settings, deletes all of it permanently. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on request and nothing we could restore for you.

Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases of in-game gold and booster bundles. These are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or receive your payment card, bank details, billing address, or Google account. The app only receives a yes/no answer from Google Play telling it whether a purchase completed, plus the localised price text to display. Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a small banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and optional "watch a video for a reward" ads that only play when you choose to tap them.

To do that, the AdMob SDK reads your device's advertising ID — a resettable identifier Android provides for exactly this purpose — along with technical information about your device such as its model, operating-system version, and coarse region. That information goes to Google, not to us. We never receive it, and we cannot link it to you.

Google's use of it is covered by the Google Privacy & Terms. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads; doing so does not affect the game or your progress.

(Browser versions of the game published on web game portals show ads served by that portal under its own privacy policy; this document covers the Google Play app.)

Internet access

The game itself makes no network requests of its own: all levels, artwork, sound and logic are bundled inside the app. Network access is used by two Google components only — Google Play Billing, to complete a purchase, and Google AdMob, to fetch ads. The app declares INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.android.vending.BILLING and the advertising-ID permissions that the AdMob SDK requires.

Children

This game is not directed at children. We build no profile of any user, of any age, and we hold no personal data at all. Ads are served by Google AdMob under Google's own policies for the ad content it serves.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds anything that collects data — analytics or advertising, for example — this page will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.

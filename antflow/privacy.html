Privacy Policy

Crab Cove: Pixel Puzzle — published by NXVTeam.
Last updated: 13 August 2026.

This game is played inside a game portal (for example CrazyGames). This page explains what the game itself collects. The portal you are playing on collects its own data under its own privacy policy, which is separate from this one.

The short version

  • The game does not ask for, or collect, your name, e-mail address, phone number or postal address.
  • The game does not track your location.
  • The game does not sell personal data to anyone.
  • What it does record is how the game is played — which level you reached, whether you won or lost, how long a level took — so the game can be made better.

What is collected, and why

DataWhy
Gameplay events — level started, won or lost, how long it took, boosters used, which advert placements were shown To see where the game is too hard, too slow or broken, and to fix it.
Game progress — current level, coins, lives, boosters, settings So you can carry on where you stopped. Stored by the portal's own save system, or in your browser's local storage.
Technical information — device type, browser, screen size, approximate country, an anonymous identifier generated by the analytics provider Collected automatically by the analytics service below. It is used to count sessions and to tell one device apart from another; it is not used to identify you as a person.

Who else receives this data

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) to record the gameplay events described above. Google processes that data as described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

The game portal (for example CrazyGames) serves the adverts and handles advertising consent itself, at the page level, outside the game. Adverts are not served by this game directly.

Cookies and local storage

The game stores your progress and settings on your own device. The analytics service may also store a small anonymous identifier there. Clearing your browser's storage for the portal's site removes both — including your saved progress.

Children

This game is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. No account is required and no personal details are requested at any point. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this game, please contact us using the address below and it will be deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA or the UK, or the CCPA in California) you may ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be deleted, or object to its processing. Because the game does not collect anything that identifies you personally, we normally cannot link stored data to an individual — but if you contact us with details of your device and roughly when you played, we will do what we can to locate and delete the relevant records.

To stop analytics entirely, you can play with an ad or tracker blocker enabled, or clear the portal site's storage in your browser. The game remains fully playable either way.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect in the game.

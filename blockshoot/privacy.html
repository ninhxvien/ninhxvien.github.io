Privacy Policy

Block Shoot! — published by NXVTeam.
Last updated: 31 August 2026.

This policy covers the Block Shoot! Android app (com.game.blockshoot) on Google Play.

The short version

  • The game does not ask for, or collect, your name, e-mail address, phone number or postal address.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. Nothing to register, nothing to log in to.
  • The game does not track your location, and does not read your contacts, photos or files.
  • The game does not sell personal data to anyone.
  • Your progress is saved on your own device, not on our servers. We do not have servers.
  • What is collected is how the game is played, crash reports, and an advertising identifier used to show adverts.

What is collected, and why

DataWhy
In-app activity — which level you start, win, lose or retry, how much of the level you cleared, which booster you used, and when an advert was shown To see which levels are too hard, too easy or broken, and to fix them.
Crash reports and diagnostics — the error, where in the code it happened, device model, Android version, app version So a crash can be found and fixed. These reports are generated automatically when the app stops unexpectedly.
Advertising ID — the resettable identifier Google provides on your device Used by Google AdMob to select and count adverts, and to limit how often you see the same one. It is a device identifier, not your name.
Game progress — levels cleared, stars, gold, the ball and cannon upgrades you own, boosters, and your sound and vibration settings So you can carry on where you stopped. Stored on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Adverts

The app shows adverts through Google AdMob: an advert at a break between levels, and optional adverts you choose to watch in exchange for a reward, such as extra shots or a booster. There is no banner advert. Google uses the advertising identifier described above to select those adverts. What Google does with it is described in How Google uses data when you use our partners' apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before any advert is requested, using Google's own consent form. You can change that choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy & adverts. If you decline, the game keeps working and no adverts are requested.

You can also reset or delete the advertising identifier at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device, and turn off advert personalisation there.

Purchases

The app sells packs of in-game gold, and a one-off purchase that removes the adverts shown between levels. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play — the game never sees, receives or stores your card number, billing address or any other payment detail. Google tells the app only that a purchase succeeded, along with an order identifier.

That order identifier is stored on your device for one reason: so that if the app is closed at the wrong moment, the same purchase is not credited to you twice. It is not sent anywhere. Refunds and purchase history are handled by Google Play, not by us.

Who else receives this data

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) for the in-app activity and crash reports above, and Google AdMob for adverts. Google processes that data as described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

Apart from Google, and apart from Google Play handling payments, the data is not shared with anyone, and it is not sold.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, gold, upgrades, settings and the purchase order identifiers are stored in the app's own storage on your device. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, removes all of it — including your saved progress, which cannot be recovered afterwards.

Children

This game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over, and is not directed at children under 13. No account is required and no personal details are requested at any point. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this game, please contact us at the address below and it will be deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA or the UK, or the CCPA in California) you may ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be deleted, or object to its processing. Because the game collects nothing that identifies you as a person, we normally cannot link stored records to an individual — but if you contact us with your device model and roughly when you played, we will do what we can to locate and delete the relevant records.

To stop advert-related collection, decline consent in the game or reset the advertising identifier in your Android settings as described above. To remove everything the game has stored, uninstall the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect in the app.
